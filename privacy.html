Privacy Policy.

We collect only what we need to do the work. We never sell your information. Ask and we delete it.

Effective October 24, 2026. Ridgeline Local · ridgelinemarketinghq@gmail.com · 150 Sedgewood Dr, Smithville, TN 37166

  1. What we collect

    • What you type into our forms: business name, town, type of business, email, phone, services, Google profile link.
    • Your plan and billing status from Stripe (we never see your full card number).
    • Emails you send us, and the results of your AI checks and reports.
    • For clients: leads we find for you, and customer emails you choose to give us for review requests.

  2. How we use it

    To run your free AI check, deliver your plan, send your reports and leads, email your customers review requests you asked for, answer you, and bill you. That’s it.

  3. Who helps us

    Stripe (payments), Google (Gmail, for our email), FormSubmit (delivers website forms to our inbox), OpenAI and Google Gemini (we ask them general questions such as “best barbers in your town”; we don’t send them your personal contact details), and GitHub (hosts this website). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

  4. What we never do

    We never sell or rent your information or your customers’ information, and we never use your customers’ emails for anything except the review requests you asked for.

  5. How long we keep it

    While you’re a client, and up to 2 years after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Billing records may be kept longer where the law requires.

  6. Your choices

    Email ridgelinemarketinghq@gmail.com to see, fix or delete your information, or to stop emails. Anyone who receives an email from us can reply “stop” and won’t hear from us again.
