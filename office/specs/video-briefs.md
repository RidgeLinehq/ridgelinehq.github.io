# Video briefs (Nova writes, Grayson films)

Grayson's plan (Oct 22, 2026): face-to-camera commercials for Instagram Reels and Facebook,
in a suit, with pop-ups on screen ("tap here", AI answer screens, numbers). The office writes
the brief; Grayson films on his phone and assembles in CapCut (free); the office writes the
caption and the posting plan. Nothing is published by the crew; Grayson posts. Hometown
promotion is his personal Facebook; Ridgeline's own accounts (Instagram @digitalcreationsbyridge
to be renamed, a Ridgeline Facebook Page) carry the same videos.

## Format (20 to 40 seconds, vertical 9:16, captions on)
| Second | On camera (Grayson says) | Pop-up (Nova writes the exact text) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-2 | The loss, as a hook. "Your competitor just got recommended by ChatGPT." | none (face, eye contact) |
| 2-8 | The demo. "Watch. 'Best roofer in Cabot, Arkansas.'" | mock AI answer card: 3 competitor names, "Your business: not mentioned" (names from a real check Scout ran; marked Example on screen) |
| 8-16 | Why. "45% of people used AI to find a local business last year. AI names businesses with reviews, photos and a complete profile." | "45%" big; "133 reviews vs 11" |
| 16-26 | What I do. "I fix the signals, I ask your real customers for reviews, and every month I send you the screenshot." | checklist: Google profile · reviews · Yelp · monthly proof |
| 26-34 | Risk gone + CTA. "No contract, no password. Free AI check first. Link in bio." | arrow to "Free AI check → ridgelinehq.github.io" |

## Brief fields (drafts/<id>, kind "script")
title · hook (the exact first sentence) · lines (array of {second, say, popup}) · caption (under
150 chars, plain, one CTA) · hashtags (5 to 8, local + trade) · wardrobe/setting note (suit,
clean background, daylight) · pop-up assets (text and colors: navy #0b141d, orange #ff8a2a,
white) · CapCut steps (3 lines) · postPlan {platform, day, time} · status: brief → filmed →
posted (Grayson taps) · results {views, clicks} (Grayson types; Sage reads).

## Rules
- The AI answer on screen is a real check (Scout's) and is labeled Example; never a fake
  screenshot passed off as ChatGPT.
- Numbers only from specs/offer.md and the site's cited studies.
- One video per brief, two briefs a week (Monday and Thursday runs), never more unless Grayson
  asks. Each brief must differ from the last three (different hook, different town, different
  trade).
- Honest, confident, no hype words (shared/rules.md list). Sounds like Grayson talking to one
  owner, not an ad read.
