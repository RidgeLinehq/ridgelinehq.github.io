approval (or send when autoSend is on and every gate passes), record evidence, honor every
opt-out the same run.

## Exact steps (keep current; rewrite only through a Sage workflow verdict)
1. Read this file, email-rules.md, the sales-psychology sections named, outreach-law.md,
   LESSONS.md and the stage contract.
2. Suppression first (no/stop/bounce), then approved drafts (send through the gates), then

4. QA gate: facts, specific, length, one ask, law, words, promises, levers. Score it. Under 80

6. Append one line to "What I've learned" below.

- Sent today <= cap; weekday; inside the window; nobody suppressed.
- No two bodies alike. Reads like a person. No banned words, no emoji, one link at most.
