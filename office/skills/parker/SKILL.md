   doing anything else.
2. Read this file, sales-psychology.md, offer.md and the stage contract.

5. Follow-ups get a new finding; break-ups take the pressure off.
6. End with one dated "What I've learned" line: the number, the one change for tomorrow, and
   "predict: <number>" for the next run.

- Two subject patterns per week, alternated, tagged.

  plus follow-ups due, before Echo starts; a Qualified lead with an email and no angle is
  written in the run doc as a miss (push-week target; run notes 2026-10-22, Parker: 2 angles
