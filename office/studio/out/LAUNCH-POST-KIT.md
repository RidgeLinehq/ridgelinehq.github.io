**The engagement mechanic**

The lever, in every pinned comment: "Comment your trade and your town. I'll ask ChatGPT and reply here with who it named."

Why this and not likes, follows, duets or giveaways: it manufactures, honestly, the three signals all three platforms weight most for a zero-follower post: comments from strangers, creator replies (each reply is a new comment plus a notification that pulls the owner back to the video for a second view), and shares (owners tag a friend in the same trade to compare). It turns one video into dozens of town-specific proof lines sitting in public, each written for one owner about his town: the office's email opener (their loss, first line; give first) done where other owners can read it. Every word is a real, dated check, so it passes the Honesty rules without a workaround, and the office already has the machine: the aicheck Action.
