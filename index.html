What you get

The work, in plain words.

No jargon, no mystery reports. Here is exactly what we do.

01

Google Business Profile cleanup

We go through your categories, services, description, hours and photos, then send you every fix written out, ready to paste (about 10 minutes). We check that the changes went live and keep a record.

02

AI visibility report

Every week we ask ChatGPT and Gemini the questions your customers ask. Once a month you get a short report: are you named, who else is, and how that changed.

03

Review requests to your real customers

Send us your customers’ emails and we send each one a short, friendly review request from your business. You also get a ready-to-send text and a QR card for the counter. We never write or buy reviews.

04

AI Lead Finder Growth + Pro

We look for people asking for your kind of business in your area, only in places that allow it: public posts through official tools, messages on your Google profile, and your own lead form. Then we send them to you.

05

Lead tracking Growth + Pro

Every lead we send is logged with the date, where it came from, the contact info and its status. Once a month we ask you, “Did this lead turn into a job?” and keep the score.

06

Your own lead page Pro

A simple page for your business with a lead form and your own tracking link. Every form lead is counted and emailed to you right away.

Questions

Quick answers.

Who is this for?
Any local business: restaurants, salons, barbers, gyms, dentists, contractors, cleaners, auto shops and more. If customers look you up on Google or ask an AI who to call, this is for you.
What exactly is the free AI check?
We ask ChatGPT and Gemini the question your customers ask, like “Who are the best barbers in your town?”, and email you their exact answers, whether you were named, which competitors were, and the three fixes we’d make first. Free, no card, no call.
How much of my time does it take?
About 5 minutes to fill in the short form after you sign up, and about 10 minutes to paste in your Google profile fixes (we write them for you). Everything else happens by email.
Do I have to give you my password?
No. Never. If you want, you can add us as a manager on your Google profile; your password stays yours.
Where do the leads come from?
Only from places that allow it: public posts where people ask for local recommendations (found through official tools), messages and calls on your own Google profile, and your own lead form (Pro). We never buy lists, scrape private groups or spam anyone. Every lead is logged, so you always see exactly what you got.
What if it doesn’t work for me?
Growth and Pro come with the Second Month Free Guarantee: if your first month brings zero tracked leads and no improvement in your AI visibility, your second month is free. No arguing. And every plan is month to month.
Do you write or buy reviews?
Never. We only send review requests to your real customers and let them say what they want.
How do I cancel?
Reply “cancel” to any email from us. No contract, no fee.
