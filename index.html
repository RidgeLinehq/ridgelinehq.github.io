A few useful answers

Clear before you start.

Who is Ridgeline Local for?
Independent roofing companies and tree services that want their online business information, review process, and local profiles to better reflect the work they do.
How much of my time does this take?
We start with your business details and agree on the priorities. You provide any needed business information, grant appropriate manager access, and approve work when required. We keep the process moving by email.
Do I need to share my password?
You keep your passwords. Where supported, you add us with the appropriate manager access, including for your Google Business Profile.
Do you buy or write reviews?
Never. We give you a simple way to ask real customers for honest feedback right after a job. Fake or gated reviews break platform rules and can get a profile suspended — the opposite of what you’re paying for.
How does this relate to AI recommendations?
We strengthen the public business information AI tools may find when answering local questions, then document sample checks in ChatGPT and Gemini. Answers vary by question, location, and time; your report shows what was checked and what changed.
What will I actually receive each month?
The agreed profile and listing work, weekly Google post drafts, and a monthly proof report showing completed tasks, review growth, documented AI checks, and the next priorities.
Do I have to sign up after the free check?
The check gives you screenshots and three recommended fixes. Review them first, then decide whether you want our help putting the next steps into practice.
