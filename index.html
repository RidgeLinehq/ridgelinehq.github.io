Is this legit?
Yes. No contract, and you can cancel anytime by replying to any email. Growth and Pro come with a second month free guarantee. You see the real ChatGPT and Gemini answers before and after, and every lead we send. If it’s not for you, you stop. No fee, no hassle.
What’s in the free AI check?
We ask ChatGPT and Gemini who they recommend for your kind of business in your town, then email you their exact answers, whether you were named, and the three fixes we’d make first. Free. No card, no call.
How much of my time does it take?
About five minutes for the signup form, and about ten minutes to paste in your Google profile fixes. We write them for you. The rest happens by email.

No. Never. If you like, you can add us as a manager on your Google profile. Your login stays yours.

On Pro, we look for people asking for your kind of business nearby: public posts asking for recommendations, messages on your own Google profile, and your own lead form. Every lead is logged. We never buy lists or message strangers in your name.
How do I cancel?
Reply “cancel” to any email from us. That’s it. No contract, no fee, and you keep the service through the month you paid for.
