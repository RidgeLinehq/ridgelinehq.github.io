Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend me?
No — and anyone who says they can is selling you something. AI answers change by question, town and time. What we control is the information those tools find about you, and we show you exactly what changed each month.
Do you buy or write reviews?
Never. We give you a simple way to ask real customers for honest feedback right after a job. Fake or gated reviews break platform rules and can get a profile suspended — the opposite of what you're paying for.
Do I have to give you my password?
No. You add Ridgeline as a manager on your Google Business Profile — two taps, and you can remove us any time. Never send passwords by email to anyone.
Do I have to get on a call?
No. The whole service runs by email so it fits around jobs. If something needs clarifying, we email you a short question.
What does the free AI check actually include?
Screenshots of what ChatGPT and Gemini say when asked for your trade in your town, plus the three fixes we'd make first. It's yours whether or not you hire us.
How fast will I see something change?
Profile and listing fixes go live within the first two weeks. Reviews build from your first jobs after the kit arrives. Your first full report lands 30 days after you start.
Is Ridgeline only for roofers and tree services?
For now, yes. Focusing on two trades means the checks, review scripts and posts are built for how your customers actually search. If you're a different trade, email us anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether we're a fit.
