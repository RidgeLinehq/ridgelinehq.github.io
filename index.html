Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommends me?

No. Nobody honestly can. AI answers change by query, location, and time. We improve the information available about your business and show what changed.

Do you buy or fake reviews?

Never. We help you ask real customers for honest feedback. Fake reviews and review manipulation can violate platform rules and applicable law.

Do I need to give you my password?

No. Where supported, you add us as a Manager to your Google Business Profile. Never send passwords by email.

Do I have to get on calls?

No. The process is designed to run by email. If a detail needs clarification, we email you.

Will this put me first in AI results?

We don't promise rankings or placement. The goal is to strengthen your profiles, listings, and review process and then measure visibility over time.

Is the free AI check a commitment?

No. We'll send the screenshots and three recommended fixes. You decide whether you want help implementing them.
