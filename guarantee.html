Written terms

Review-growth guarantee.

If you don’t get at least 15 new genuine reviews in 60 days, month 3 is free.

These are the full terms of that promise. Effective October 22, 2026. Ridgeline Local · ridgelinemarketinghq@gmail.com

  1. Who it covers

    Every client on the founding client plan ($147/month for the first 3 months). It is included automatically — nothing extra to sign up for or pay.

  2. When the 60 days start

    Day 1 is the day your first payment goes through. The 60 days end at the end of day 60.

  3. How reviews are counted

    We write down the review total on your Google Business Profile on day 1 and send it to you by email. On day 60 we check the total again. New reviews = the day-60 total minus the day-1 total.

  4. What counts as a genuine review

    A review on your Google Business Profile, posted during the 60 days, by a real customer about their real experience with your business.

    • Reviews that were bought, written by you or your staff, or given in exchange for a discount or gift don’t count.
    • Reviews Google removes or hides don’t count.
    • Good, bad, or in between — every genuine review counts. We never ask customers for only positive reviews.

  5. What we ask of you

    Use the review request kit we give you: share the short message or QR code with your customers after completed jobs. Asking real customers is what makes the reviews happen — that part has to come from you and your crew.

  6. How “month 3 is free” works

    If the count on day 60 is under 15, we refund your month 3 payment in full to the card you paid with, within 7 days of that payment. Your service keeps running as normal. You can also cancel at any time and still receive the refund if you qualified.

  7. How to claim it

    You don’t have to. We send you the day-60 count by email either way, and if you qualify, the refund is automatic. If you think we counted wrong, reply to that email with what you see and we’ll recheck it together.

  8. What it doesn’t promise

    This guarantee is about review growth only. It is not a promise of rankings, AI recommendations, leads, calls, or jobs.

Questions about these terms?

Email ridgelinemarketinghq@gmail.com and we’ll answer in plain words.

The 15-review, 60-day promise on our website and these terms say the same thing; these terms just explain how it works. If anything here is unclear, the reading that’s better for you, the client, applies.
