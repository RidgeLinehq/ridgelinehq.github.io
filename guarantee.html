Second Month Free Guarantee.

If your first month brings you zero tracked leads AND no improvement in your AI visibility, your second month is free. No arguing.

These are the full terms. Updated October 27, 2026. Ridgeline Local · ridgelinemarketinghq@gmail.com

  1. Who it covers

    Every client on the Growth ($75/month) or Pro ($125/month) plan, for their first month. It’s included automatically. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to ask for.

  2. Your starting score (day 1)

    When you sign up, we run your AI visibility check and email you the result. We ask ChatGPT and Gemini three questions a customer would ask about your kind of business in your town, so that’s 6 answers. For each answer you get:

    • 1 point if it names your business among the first three businesses it mentions,
    • ½ point if it names you further down,
    • 0 if it doesn’t name you.

    Your score = points ÷ 6 × 100, so it runs from 0 to 100. We save the exact answers so you can read them.

  3. What counts as a tracked lead

    A real person who asked for or contacted your business through something we track during your first month (day 1 to day 28):

    • a lead our Lead Finder sent you (Pro): a public post or request from someone looking for what you do,
    • a message or call notice from your Google Business Profile (once you add us as a manager or forward them to us),
    • a form on your Ridgeline lead page (Pro).

    Spam, duplicates, and tests don’t count. Every lead is logged with the date, the source and the contact info, and you can ask us for the list any time.

  4. End of month one: the check

    On day 28 (counted from your first payment, so it’s done before your second month is billed) we run the exact same AI check again and count your tracked leads. If you have zero tracked leads AND your score is not higher than your day-1 score, the guarantee applies.

  5. How it’s paid

    We put a 100%-off credit on your second month in our payment system (Stripe), so you aren’t charged for it. If the charge already went through, we refund it in full. Either way we email you the same day it’s applied.

  6. What we ask of you

    Fill in the short onboarding form after you sign up (business name, Google profile link, phone, services, city) so we can run your check and start the work. That’s it.

  7. Why we offer this

    We’d rather show you results than ask you to trust us. If your first month shows nothing on either count, you don’t pay for the second.
